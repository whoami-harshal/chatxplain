# ChatXplain Privacy Policy

*Effective 23 October 2026*

ChatXplain is an app on the Anna platform. It reads a chat screenshot you choose and suggests what the other person most likely meant, along with some reply ideas. This page explains what happens to your data.

## What ChatXplain processes

When you tap **Decode**, ChatXplain sends Anna's AI:

- **Your screenshot.** It's shrunk and converted to a JPEG on your device first.
- **Your choices:** who the person is (for example "Friend") and which side of the chat is you.
- **Your optional note** (up to 200 characters), if you wrote one.

Anna's AI reads these once to write your result.

If you then tap a **tone** (for example "Funny") to rewrite your reply ideas, ChatXplain sends the AI a short text transcript of the latest messages from that first read, not the screenshot. The transcript is kept in memory only while that result is open, and is never saved. The request runs through Anna's platform and counts toward your Anna AI usage, like any other Anna AI request.

## What ChatXplain stores

- **Screenshots: never.** ChatXplain doesn't save your screenshot anywhere: not on a server, not in your history, not in your browser storage. Once your result is shown, the app keeps no copy.
- **Recent results: text only.** To let you reopen past results, ChatXplain keeps your **last 5 text results** (vibe, summary, reply ideas, tip, date and the relationship you picked) in **your own Anna app storage**. Only you can see them.
- **Preferences on your device.** Your light/dark mode and result-language choice are saved in this device's browser storage, so the app remembers them.
- **Sensitive results aren't kept.** If a result includes a safety note (for example, the chat looked threatening), it isn't added to your history.

## How to delete your data

- Open ChatXplain and tap **Clear history** in the **Recent** section. Tap it again to confirm. This deletes all saved results.

## What ChatXplain doesn't do

- No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
- No selling or sharing of your data.
- No accounts of its own. ChatXplain uses your existing Anna account.

## Good to know

- ChatXplain gives a **best guess, not a fact**. Don't make important decisions based only on its output.
- Before uploading, blur names, phone numbers and other personal details you don't want to share.
- Only upload chats you're comfortable sharing with an AI.

## Children

ChatXplain is not intended for children under 13.
